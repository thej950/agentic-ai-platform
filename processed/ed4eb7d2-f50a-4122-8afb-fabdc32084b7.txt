ABC Technologies Pvt. Ltd. Employee Handbook & HR Policy Version: 1.0 Effective Date: January 2026

Company Overview Welcome to ABC Technologies Pvt. Ltd. We are a technology consulting company specializing in Cloud Computing, Artificial Intelligence, Data Engineering, DevOps, and Software Development. Our mission is to deliver innovative and secure technology solutions while providing employees with a collaborative and growth-oriented workplace. This Employee Handbook outlines the policies, guidelines, and expectations applicable to all employees. Every employee is expected to read, understand, and comply with these policies throughout their employment.

Vision To become a trusted global technology partner by delivering innovative digital solutions and creating a workplace that encourages learning, collaboration, and excellence. Mission • Deliver high-quality technology solutions. • Maintain customer satisfaction through innovation. • Provide continuous learning opportunities for employees. • Ensure a safe, inclusive, and respectful work environment. • Promote ethical business practices and teamwork.

Code of Conduct Employees are expected to maintain professional behavior at all times. Every employee should treat colleagues, customers, vendors, and business partners with dignity and respect. Employees must: • Maintain honesty and integrity.

• Follow all company policies. • Respect diversity and inclusion. • Protect confidential information. • Avoid conflicts of interest. • Use company resources responsibly. • Maintain professional communication both internally and externally. Violation of the code of conduct may result in disciplinary action depending on the severity of the incident.

Employment Policy Employment with ABC Technologies is subject to successful completion of background verification, document verification, and compliance with company policies. Employees are expected to perform assigned responsibilities efficiently and maintain professional standards. The company reserves the right to modify policies whenever business requirements or legal regulations change.

Working Hours The standard office working schedule is: • Monday to Friday • Office Timing: 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM • Core Collaboration Hours: 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM • Lunch Break: 1 hour Employees are expected to complete eight working hours each business day.

Attendance Policy Employees should record attendance through the company attendance system before 10:00 AM. Employees arriving late should inform their reporting manager in advance whenever possible.

Repeated late attendance without valid reasons may result in counseling or disciplinary action. Attendance records are maintained electronically for payroll and performance purposes.

Leave Policy Employees are eligible for the following annual leave benefits: • Annual Leave: 18 days • Sick Leave: 12 days • Casual Leave: 8 days • Optional Holidays: 2 days Unused annual leave may be carried forward up to 10 days into the following calendar year. Sick leave extending beyond two consecutive working days requires submission of a valid medical certificate. Employees should apply for planned leave at least three working days in advance. Emergency leave should be communicated immediately to the reporting manager and HR department.

Work From Home Policy Employees may work remotely with prior approval from their reporting manager. Employees working from home should ensure: • Reliable internet connectivity. • Company-issued laptop. • Secure VPN connection. • Quiet and secure working environment. • Availability during office hours. Employees are expected to attend all scheduled meetings and remain reachable through official communication channels.

Payroll Policy Employee salaries are credited on the last working day of every month. Payslips are available through the employee self-service portal. Salary deductions including income tax, provident fund, and professional tax are processed according to applicable government regulations. Any payroll-related issues should be reported to HR within five working days after salary credit.

Performance Management Performance reviews are conducted twice each year. Employee performance is evaluated based on: • Technical knowledge • Quality of work • Communication skills • Team collaboration • Customer satisfaction • Problem-solving ability • Innovation • Ownership and accountability Managers provide regular feedback to help employees improve performance and achieve career goals.

Promotion Policy Promotions are based on multiple factors including: • Overall performance • Technical expertise • Leadership qualities • Business requirements • Customer feedback

• Organizational contribution Promotion decisions are reviewed annually by department heads and HR.

Training and Development ABC Technologies encourages continuous learning. Employees are encouraged to participate in: • Cloud certification programs • Artificial Intelligence training • Cybersecurity awareness sessions • Leadership development programs • Technical workshops • Soft skills training Training participation is monitored by the Learning and Development team.

IT Asset Policy Employees are provided company assets based on business requirements. Typical assets include: • Laptop • Charger • Headset • Security token • External monitor (where applicable) Employees are responsible for protecting company assets from damage, theft, or misuse. Lost or stolen devices must be reported immediately to the IT Helpdesk.

Information Security Policy Information security is the responsibility of every employee.

Employees must: • Keep passwords confidential. • Lock computers when away from desks. • Use approved software only. • Avoid sharing confidential documents without authorization. • Report suspected security incidents immediately. • Store sensitive information securely. Failure to comply with security policies may lead to disciplinary action.

Password Policy Passwords must: • Contain at least 12 characters. • Include uppercase letters. • Include lowercase letters. • Include numbers. • Include special characters. • Be changed every 90 days. Employees should never reuse passwords across personal and company accounts.

Email and Internet Usage Company email accounts are provided strictly for business communication. Employees should not: • Access illegal or inappropriate websites. • Download unauthorized software. • Share confidential company information externally without approval. • Use company email for personal commercial activities. Reasonable personal internet usage is permitted provided it does not interfere with work responsibilities.

Travel and Expense Reimbursement Business travel requires prior approval from the reporting manager. Eligible expenses include: • Airfare • Hotel accommodation • Taxi or local transportation • Meals during official travel • Client meeting expenses Expense claims should be submitted within ten working days along with supporting bills.

Employee Benefits Eligible employees receive various benefits including: • Health insurance • Life insurance • Provident Fund • Gratuity • Learning and certification support • Employee wellness programs • Performance bonus based on company policy Benefit eligibility may vary depending on employment type and applicable regulations.

Medical, Maternity and Paternity Leave Medical leave beyond two consecutive working days requires medical documentation. Eligible employees receive: • 26 weeks of paid maternity leave. • 10 working days of paid paternity leave.

The company supports employees during medical emergencies and encourages timely communication with HR.

Holidays The company publishes an annual holiday calendar at the beginning of each financial year. Employees are also eligible for two optional holidays that can be selected based on personal or regional preferences.

Overtime Policy Overtime should be approved by the reporting manager before additional work is performed. Compensation or compensatory leave for overtime will be provided according to company policy and applicable labor regulations.

Resignation and Notice Period Employees wishing to resign should submit their resignation through the HR portal and inform their reporting manager. The standard notice period is 90 calendar days unless otherwise specified in the employment agreement. Employees are expected to complete knowledge transfer and hand over all assigned responsibilities before their last working day.

Exit Process Before leaving the organization, employees must complete the following: • Return all company-issued assets. • Return ID card and access cards. • Complete exit interview. • Settle pending reimbursements or dues. • Complete clearance from IT, HR, Finance, and Administration departments.

Final settlement will be processed after successful completion of all exit formalities.

Anti-Harassment and Equal Opportunity ABC Technologies maintains a workplace free from harassment, discrimination, bullying, and retaliation. Employment decisions are based solely on qualifications, skills, performance, and business needs. The company is committed to providing equal employment opportunities to all individuals regardless of gender, religion, age, disability, nationality, or any other legally protected characteristic.

Confidentiality and Data Privacy Employees must maintain strict confidentiality regarding: • Customer information • Source code • Business strategies • Financial information • Internal documents • Product roadmaps Confidentiality obligations continue even after employment ends. Employees handling personal or customer information must comply with applicable data privacy regulations and company security standards.

Emergency Procedures During emergencies, employees should: • Follow evacuation instructions. • Contact emergency response teams when necessary. • Assemble at designated safe locations. • Report injuries immediately.

• Cooperate with emergency coordinators until normal operations resume. Regular emergency drills may be conducted to ensure employee preparedness.

The office operates from 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday through Friday. Q3. When is salary credited? Salary is credited on the last working day of each month. Q4. Can employees work from home? Yes. Employees may work remotely with manager approval while following company security guidelines. Q5. How often are performance reviews conducted? Performance reviews are conducted twice every year. Q6. What is the notice period? The standard notice period is 90 calendar days. Q7. What company assets are provided? Employees may receive a laptop, charger, headset, security token, and other equipment based on business requirements. Q8. What is the password requirement? Passwords must contain at least 12 characters with uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters. Q9. Who is covered under the health insurance plan? The standard health insurance plan covers the employee and eligible dependents as defined by company policy. Q10. How can employees report security incidents? Security incidents should be reported immediately to the IT Security Team or Helpdesk through official communication channels.
